Monero seed word lists in Chinese (simplified), Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish
(src/mnemonics/chinese_simplified.h, japanese.h, portuguese.h and spanish.h in
https://github.com/monero-project/monero). Used by CheckLink only to recognise a Monero
recovery phrase in a code, so that it can hide it and warn.

Each of the four files says that its word list was originally created by dabura667 and
released under the MIT licence, and that the code around the list is the Monero Project's
(BSD-3-Clause, licences/Monero-BSD-3-Clause.txt). The notice below is copied unchanged from
those files, without the comment markers.
