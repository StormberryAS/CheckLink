First release, and an early one: 0.0.1 is a functioning prototype, so expect gaps and changes. CheckLink shows what a link or a QR code holds, marks the tracking parts its dated rules name with who learns them, and gives you a clean link to copy, share or open, or a QR code of it. It reads QR codes from a picture, a share or the camera. One permission, the camera, used only while you scan. No internet access. If a clean link does not work, use the original; Email us opens a message to tell us.
