0.0.2 is a maintenance release. The QR code reader moves to zxing-wasm 3.1.6, which brings the latest zxing-cpp fixes and tidier memory use while it reads. The Android WebView library moves to androidx.webkit 1.17.1. How CheckLink works does not change: still one permission, the camera, used only while you scan, and no internet access. Full release log: https://github.com/StormberryAS/CheckLink/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
