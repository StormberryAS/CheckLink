Specific to CheckLink

It is a second look, not a verdict. CheckLink shows what a link or a QR code holds and points out signs worth checking. It does not, and cannot, tell you whether a site, a sender or an offer is genuine.

It removes only what is on its list. CheckLink removes the parts of a link that its own rules name. The rules carry a date, shown at the foot of the page as "Rules dated". A tracking part the rules do not name stays in the link.

Cleaning can break a link. Some sites need a part that looks like tracking. Every removal can be undone: untick the part and the clean link changes with it. If a clean link does not work, use the original.

It cannot see where short links go. CheckLink never opens a link, so it cannot follow a short link or a redirect to where it ends up. It says so when it finds one.

"No warning signs found" is not a verdict. A link with nothing to point out can still lead somewhere harmful. Decide for yourself before you open it.

Payment details are shown, never paid. When a QR code holds a payment request, CheckLink shows what is in it and never pays anything. Check who gets paid and how much in your own bank or wallet app before you pay.
